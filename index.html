Why Charge ₹50/Day? Full Justification For All

We refuse to offer "free" gimmicks that waste human potential. Here is why the commitment deposit exists:

1. Eliminates Casual Ghosters (Skin in the Game)

When an internship is completely free, 85%+ of candidates register impulsively and abandon it after Day 2. That poisons cohort momentum. A tiny ₹50/day skin-in-the-game demands real personal accountability and ensures you show up every single day.

2. Guarding Strictly 50 Exclusive Seats

This cohort is strictly capped at 50 selected interns. If an uncommitted person signs up for free and quits after 3 days, they have permanently robbed a seat from a hardworking student who genuinely needed this mentorship.

3. Mutual Dignity & Respect for Mentor Time

Kapil (The One Rupee Full-Stack CEO) and our senior engineering mentors spend dedicated hours reviewing code, inspecting pull requests, and providing 1-on-1 career guidance. Mutual dignity requires that the intern also values that time.

4. Real Human Evaluation & Infrastructure

Every daily deliverable is inspected by human evaluators, not an automated grading bot. Your code is verified, your GitHub commits audited, and your credentials cryptographically stamped. Real evaluation requires real operational bandwidth.

5. ₹50 INR Daily: Less Than A Cup of Chai

We do not charge ₹10,000 or ₹25,000 boot camp tuition. At ₹50/day, it is less than the cost of a daily canteen snack. If a learner cannot invest ₹50 daily in their own professional future, they are not ready for the rigors of the tech industry.

Zero Cost for High Performers

For any intern who takes this seriously, learns, builds their capstone, and earns a merit stipend, the entire ₹1,000 is 100% refunded back. The program becomes 100% cost-free with huge positive cash rewards!
